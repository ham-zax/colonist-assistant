## Colonist artwork and compatibility evidence

Colonist Assistant does not bundle Colonist card or piece artwork into the
extension. While a game is open, it may display the same SVG/PNG asset URLs
already published by Colonist to the current page. Accepted URLs are restricted
to Colonist’s official asset paths.

`docs/colonist-evidence/` contains versioned copies of public Colonist client
assets retained strictly as compatibility/rule-audit evidence. They are not
part of the extension build or release artifact, are not offered under this
project's MIT license, and remain the property of their respective copyright
holders. The evidence manifest records original public source URLs and hashes.
